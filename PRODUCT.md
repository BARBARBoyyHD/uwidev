# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, Framer Motion, OGL, lucide-react. Monorepo structure under `apps/`.

## Users

- **Primary:** Business owners experiencing operational friction from manual processes, disconnected systems, or generic software that doesn't fit their workflow. Restaurants, retail, logistics, professional services, startups.
- **Secondary:** Startup / operations teams needing custom internal or customer-facing software (dashboards, tracking, booking, e-commerce, mobile apps).
- **Secondary:** International prospects seeking a capable software development partner.

## Product Purpose

Company profile website for Uwi_Dev. Primary objective is lead generation — make a potential customer comfortable enough to start a conversation about a software project. Communicates what Uwi_Dev does, which problems it solves, how it works, and why it's trustworthy.

Success: a new visitor can quickly answer what Uwi_Dev does, who it's for, what it can build, how it works, what makes it different, and how to make contact.

## Positioning

> Software built around the way your business works.

Uwi_Dev is a founder-led software engineering studio. Small by design. Serious about engineering. Differentiation comes from understanding the business problem first and building technology around it — not forcing businesses into generic software.

## Operating Context

Project lifecycle: Discovery → Architecture → Development → Deployment → Ongoing Engineering.

Fixed project pricing model, initially targeting Rp5–10 million range, open to different sizes when appropriate. No internal engineering team required on the client side.

## Capabilities and Constraints

**Confirmed capabilities to communicate on the site:**
- Business systems (inventory, sales, reporting, HR, operations)
- Web applications (custom workflows)
- Mobile applications (customer, employee, operations, field teams)
- System integrations (APIs, payment gateways, WhatsApp, ERP, internal platforms)

**Constraints:**
- No fabricated clients, testimonials, statistics, certifications, awards, or team members
- No fake client logos or pretend large-organization positioning
- Credibility must come from clarity, engineering knowledge, process, and quality of presentation
- Initial version may not have real case studies — section must support future content without redesign

**Design constraint:**
- Dark-to-Light Security Console visual concept (dark hero → calm daylight body)
- Anti-AI-slop: no generic templates, gradient text, glassmorphism, excessive glow, stock imagery

## Brand Commitments

- **Name:** Uwi_Dev
- **Voice:** Conversational, direct, confident, human, technically credible, easy to understand
- **Tone principle:** Business first. Technology second.
- **Personality:** Professional enough to trust. Personal enough to talk to.
- **Founder-led identity:** Direct communication, collaboration, practical engineering, fewer organizational layers, direct involvement from the people building the software.

## Evidence on Hand

- BRD.md (comprehensive business requirements)
- DESIGN.md (Dark-to-Light Security Console design system)
- AGENTS.md (engineering constitution)
- Existing codebase: 18 components, i18n support, full page structure, animations
- No real case studies, testimonials, or client logos yet — must not be fabricated

## Product Principles

1. **Clarity over decoration.** Every element earns its place through purpose, not trend.
2. **Honesty as credibility.** Never fabricate business claims. Quality of presentation replaces quantity of proof.
3. **Business understanding first.** The website should make visitors feel understood before it asks them to trust.
4. **Technical depth without jargon.** Engineering competence communicated through clarity, not complexity.
5. **Growth-ready architecture.** Structure supports future case studies, testimonials, services, and content without redesign.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Sufficient color contrast (AA at 16px+)
- Keyboard accessibility with visible focus states (2px accent-blue outline)
- Semantic HTML and heading hierarchy
- Accessible form labels
- Reduced-motion support
- Responsive across desktop, tablet, mobile
