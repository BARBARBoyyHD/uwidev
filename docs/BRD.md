# Zavron Company Profile Website

## Business Requirements Document (BRD)

**Document Version:** 1.0
**Project:** Zavron Company Profile Website
**Document Type:** Business Requirements Document
**Status:** Draft / Initial Baseline
**Primary Platform:** Responsive Web
**Primary CTA:** Contact Us

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Zavron requires a professional company profile website that establishes its identity as a **small, founder-led software engineering studio** specializing in custom business systems, web applications, mobile applications, and system integrations.

The website is not intended to function merely as an informational company profile.

Its primary purpose is to communicate:

1. What Zavron does.
2. Which business problems Zavron solves.
3. Why businesses should work with Zavron.
4. How Zavron approaches software development.
5. What types of businesses Zavron can work with.
6. How potential clients can start a conversation.

The website must balance **technical credibility with human friendliness**.

The intended perception is:

> **“These people understand technology, but they also understand my business.”**

The website should ultimately make a potential customer comfortable enough to contact Zavron about a software project.

---

# 2. Business Background

## 2.1 Company

**Zavron**

Zavron is a software engineering studio focused on building software around the operational needs of businesses.

The company focuses on custom solutions rather than forcing businesses into generic software products.

---

## 2.2 Business Model

Zavron primarily operates using a **fixed project pricing model**.

The intended project lifecycle is:

> Discovery → Architecture → Development → Deployment → Ongoing Engineering

The company is initially targeting projects in approximately the **Rp5–10 million range**, while remaining open to projects of different sizes when the problem and opportunity are appropriate.

---

# 3. Business Problem

Many businesses rely on:

* manual processes
* disconnected systems
* spreadsheets
* generic software
* repetitive operational work
* systems that do not match their actual workflow

Businesses may recognize that they need software but lack an internal engineering team to design and build it.

Existing software products may also force businesses to change their processes to match the software.

This creates operational friction.

### Core business problem

> Businesses need software that fits the way they actually work, but often lack the engineering capability to build that software themselves.

---

# 4. Business Opportunity

Zavron can provide businesses with access to professional software engineering without requiring them to establish an internal engineering team.

The opportunity is to position Zavron as:

> **A software engineering partner that understands the business problem first and builds the technology around it.**

This creates differentiation from:

* cheap freelance developers
* generic web-development agencies
* outsourcing companies
* generic SaaS products
* large enterprise consultancies

---

```text

       ↓
Complexity
       ↓
System
       ↓

       ↓
```

# 14. Design Principles

## DR-01 — Human Designed

The website must not resemble an automatically generated SaaS template.

---

## DR-02 — Restrained

Premium design should be communicated through:

* typography
* spacing
* composition
* hierarchy
* precision

not through excessive effects.

---

## DR-03 — Purposeful Visuals

Every visual element must have a purpose.

If removing it does not reduce:

* understanding
* credibility
* hierarchy
* interaction

remove it.

---

## DR-04 — Editorial Composition

Avoid repetitive component layouts.

Use asymmetry where appropriate.

---

## DR-05 — Technical + Friendly

The design should simultaneously communicate:

**Engineering competence**

and

**Human accessibility**

---
